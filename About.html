About BitDash

Founded in 2026, BitDash is dedicated to making Bitcoin accessible to everyone through high-security protocols and a user-friendly interface.

Bitcoin (BTC) is a decentralized, peer-to-peer digital currency created in 2008 by Satoshi Nakamoto, operating without a central bank. It uses blockchain technology for secure transactions, with a total supply capped at 21 million coins. As of February 2026, it is valued at ~per coin, often functioning as a digital store of value.

Key Aspects of Bitcoin:
Decentralization: No government or entity controls the Bitcoin network.
Technology: Uses blockchain, a public, decentralized, and immutable ledger to record transactions.
Mining: Computers solve complex mathematical puzzles to verify transactions and secure the network, earning new bitcoins as rewards.
Storage & Usage: Bitcoins are stored in digital wallets and can be used for purchases, investments, or peer-to-peer transfers.
Key Characteristics: Transactions are irreversible, secure, and pseudonymous.
Limit: There will only ever be 21 million bitcoins, with the final coins expected to be mined by 2140.
Regulatory Environment: Legal in many places but treated differently regarding taxes and regulations, with some regions restricting its use.
Accessibility: Accessible for purchase on exchanges, with options to buy small fractions.
Bitcoin is often referred to as "digital gold" and has become a popular investment asset, with a market capitalization of $1.35 trillion as of early 2026.
